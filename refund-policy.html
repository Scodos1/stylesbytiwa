Refund Policy

Effective Date: 14 September 2026

At Styles By Tiwa, we take pride in the quality of our handcrafted products. If you are not entirely satisfied with your purchase, we're here to help. Please read our refund and returns policy below.

1. Eligibility for Returns

You may request a return or exchange within 7 days of receiving your order, provided that:

  • The item is unused, unworn, and in its original condition.
  • The item is in its original packaging (if applicable).
  • You have proof of purchase (order confirmation via WhatsApp or email).

2. Non-Returnable Items

The following items are not eligible for return or refund:

  • Items that have been worn, washed, altered, or damaged by the customer.
  • Custom-made or made-to-measure items (unless defective).
  • Items purchased during a clearance or final sale event.
  • Items returned without prior authorisation from our team.

3. How to Request a Return

To initiate a return, please contact us via WhatsApp or email with:

  • Your order number or proof of purchase.
  • A description of the issue.
  • Photographs of the product (if applicable for defective items).

We will respond within 24–48 hours with instructions on how to proceed.

4. Refund Process

Once we receive and inspect your returned item, we will notify you of the approval or rejection of your refund. If approved:

  • Refunds will be processed within 5–10 business days.
  • Refunds will be made to your original payment method or via bank transfer, as mutually agreed.
  • Original shipping costs are non-refundable.

5. Exchanges

We are happy to exchange items for a different size or style, subject to availability. Exchange requests must be made within 7 days of delivery. The customer is responsible for any additional shipping costs for exchanges.

6. Damaged or Defective Items

If you receive a damaged or defective item, please contact us immediately (within 48 hours of delivery) with photographs and a description of the issue. We will arrange a replacement or full refund at no additional cost to you.

7. Shipping Costs for Returns

The customer is responsible for return shipping costs unless the item is defective or we made an error in fulfilling your order. We recommend using a trackable shipping service for all returns.

8. Late or Missing Refunds

If you haven't received your refund within the stated timeframe:

  • Check your bank or payment account again.
  • Contact your bank or payment provider — processing times may vary.
  • If you've done all of the above and still have not received your refund, please contact us at alagbesulait@gmail.com.
