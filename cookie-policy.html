Cookie Policy

Effective Date: 14 September 2026

This Cookie Policy explains how Styles By Tiwa uses cookies and similar technologies when you visit our website at scodos1.github.io/stylesbytiwa.

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files that are stored on your device (computer, tablet, or mobile) when you visit a website. They are widely used to make websites work efficiently and to provide information to website owners.

2. How We Use Cookies

We use a very limited number of cookies and local storage mechanisms, strictly for the following purposes:

Essential Cookies (Required)

  • Shopping Cart: We use browser local storage (not a cookie) to remember the items in your shopping bag. This is purely functional and does not track you across websites.

Third-Party Cookies

We do not knowingly set any third-party advertising or analytics cookies. However, the following third-party services loaded on our site may set their own cookies:

  • Google Fonts: Loaded from fonts.googleapis.com to display our website typography. Google may collect anonymous usage data per their Privacy Policy.
  • Google Maps: An embedded map on our contact page. Google may set cookies to track map interactions per their Privacy Policy.

3. Local Storage

In addition to cookies, we use browser local storage for:

  • Shopping cart data: Your bag items are stored locally on your device so they persist between page visits. This data never leaves your browser.
  • Cookie consent preference: We store your cookie consent choice so we don't ask you again on every visit.

4. Managing Cookies

You can control and manage cookies through your browser settings. Most browsers allow you to:

  • View what cookies are set and delete them individually.
  • Block all cookies or only third-party cookies.
  • Delete all cookies when you close your browser.

Please note that disabling cookies may affect the functionality of certain parts of our website, including the shopping cart.

5. Do Not Track

We respect the "Do Not Track" (DNT) browser setting. We do not use tracking cookies, analytics pixels, or advertising technologies that would track your activity across websites.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Cookie Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
