Styles By Tiwa

Our story / Lagos, Nigeria

Styles by Tiwa is a fashion brand based in Lagos, Nigeria, dedicated to creating stylish and comfortable clothing inspired by vintage and contemporary fashion. We specialize in unique fashion pieces such as vintage shirts, two-piece vintage sets and Greg shirts designed to help individuals express their style with confidence.

We focus on quality fabrics, creativity and attention to detail to ensure every piece stands out. Our brand also provides services ranging from unprocessed fabrics to processed fabrics, giving customers the flexibility to choose fabrics or ready to wear styles that suit their needs.

Our mission: Deliver trendy, high-quality fashion pieces that combine comfort, culture and individuality.
